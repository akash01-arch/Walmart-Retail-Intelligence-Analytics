# Walmart Inventory & Sales Intelligence Dashboard Insights

## Dashboard Business Story

Walmart generated strong sales revenue, but the business has a serious inventory and forecasting problem.  
The dashboard shows that stockouts are high, forecast error is large, and some stores/products need immediate inventory planning attention.

**Main business message:**  
Walmart’s key challenge is not only increasing sales, but improving product availability, demand forecasting, and inventory replenishment.

---

## 1. Executive Overview Insights

Walmart generated **15.26M total revenue** from **5,000 transactions** and sold **14,914 units**.  
The **average transaction value is 3,052.72**.

However, the **stockout rate is 51.86%**, which is very high. This means more than half of the records show product unavailability risk. The business should focus on reducing stockouts and improving forecasting accuracy.

### Key KPIs

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | 15.26M |
| Total Transactions | 5,000 |
| Total Quantity Sold | 14,914 |
| Average Transaction Value | 3,052.72 |
| Stockout Rate | 51.86% |
| Stockout Count | 2,593 |

### Insight Text for Dashboard

> Walmart generated 15.26M revenue from 5,000 transactions, but the 51.86% stockout rate shows serious inventory availability risk. The business should focus on reducing stockouts and improving forecasting accuracy to protect future revenue.

> Sales are driven mainly by Electronics and Appliances. Los Angeles is the strongest revenue store, while Dallas needs performance improvement. High-revenue products should be prioritized for inventory availability to avoid lost sales.

---

## 3. Inventory & Stockout Analysis Insights

The overall stockout rate is **51.86%**, which is a serious operational issue.

New York has the highest stockout rate at **55.42%**, followed by Los Angeles at **52.22%**.  
Electronics has a higher stockout rate than Appliances, which is risky because Electronics is also the highest revenue category.

### Key Inventory Findings

| Area | Insight |
|---|---:|
| Overall Stockout Rate | 51.86% |
| Highest Stockout Store | New York, NY — 55.42% |
| Lowest Stockout Store | Dallas, TX — 49.70% |
| Highest Stockout Category | Electronics — 52.88% |
| Products Below Reorder Point | 945 records |
| Inventory Lower Than Demand | 2,990 records |

### Business Meaning

Walmart is losing sales opportunities because high-demand products are not always available.  
Inventory planning should prioritize Electronics and New York store operations first.

### Insight Text for Dashboard

> Stockout risk is the biggest operational issue in this dashboard. Electronics has high revenue and high stockout risk, while New York has the highest store-level stockout rate. Walmart should prioritize replenishment for high-demand products and monitor supplier lead time more closely.

---

## 4. Forecasting Analysis Insights

Actual demand is **1,495,442**, while forecasted demand is **1,485,670**, creating a demand gap of **9,772**.

Total forecast error is **687,578**, and the forecast error rate is **45.98%**.  
This shows weak forecasting accuracy. Poor forecasting can directly increase stockouts, overstocking, and supplier planning issues.

### Key Forecasting KPIs

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Actual Demand | 1,495,442 |
| Total Forecasted Demand | 1,485,670 |
| Forecast Gap | 9,772 |
| Total Forecast Error | 687,578 |
| Forecast Error Rate | 45.98% |

### Insight Text for Dashboard

> Forecasting performance directly affects inventory planning. A 45.98% forecast error rate shows that demand prediction needs improvement. Products and stores with high forecast error should be reviewed first because poor forecasting can cause stockouts, excess inventory, and supplier planning problems.

---

## 5. Promotion & Customer Insights

Promotion activity should be aligned with inventory availability. If promotions increase demand while inventory is low, stockouts can become worse.

High-value customers and payment behavior should be monitored to understand where revenue is coming from and which customer groups should be targeted.

### Key Promotion & Customer Findings

| Area | Insight |
|---|---|
| Highest Loyalty Revenue | Platinum customers |
| Highest Revenue Age Group | 41–60 age group |
| Most Used Payment Method | Credit Card |
| Highest Revenue Payment Method | Digital Wallet |

### Insight Text for Dashboard

> Promotions should not be applied blindly. If inventory is weak, promotions can increase demand and create more stockout pressure. Walmart should target promotions toward high-value customer groups while checking product availability first.

---

## 6. Final Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize inventory replenishment for Electronics because it has the highest revenue and high stockout risk.

2. Review New York store inventory planning because it has the highest stockout rate at 55.42%.

3. Improve demand forecasting for products with high forecast error to reduce stockouts and excess inventory.

4. Monitor products below reorder point and create a reorder alert system for high-demand products.

5. Align promotions with inventory availability because promotions can increase demand and create stockout pressure.

6. Track supplier lead time regularly because longer lead times can increase stockout risk.

7. Create store-level inventory scorecards to identify locations needing operational improvement.

---

## 7. Final Dashboard Summary

The Walmart Inventory & Sales Intelligence Dashboard shows that Walmart has strong sales performance, especially in Electronics and high-performing stores like Los Angeles. However, the business faces major operational risk due to high stockout rates and weak forecasting accuracy.

The most important improvement area is inventory planning. Walmart should focus on high-demand products, high-risk stores, supplier lead times, and products with high forecast error to reduce stockouts and protect revenue.
